One disruption, five different customer outcomes

Travel disruption is rarely a one-step claim decision: customers need fast evidence capture, policy explanation and safe routing across products, events and languages. The right product experience is guidance and orchestration, not an automated claim decision.

Where JourneyBack creates value

Each benchmark routing maps to a distinct intervention. A single claim form cannot handle this range well.

  1. Explain a potential benefit and assemble the evidence path.
  2. Explain why a condition may not be met without abandoning the traveller.
  3. Ask for the missing document while the carrier interaction is still fresh.
  4. Recognise a timing or wording boundary and escalate safely.
  5. Resolve an unknown product before presenting benefit-specific guidance.
